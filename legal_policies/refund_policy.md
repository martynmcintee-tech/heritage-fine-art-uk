# UK Refund & Replacement Policy

Compliant with the UK Consumer Rights Act 2015 and Consumer Contracts Regulations.

## Damaged or Defective Items (Hassle-Free Replacement)
If your art print arrives damaged, bent, or creased during transit:
1. Contact us within 14 days of delivery via our automated support chat or email.
2. Provide your order number and a clear photo of the damage.
3. **No return required:** You do not need to queue at the Post Office to return a damaged print. Our system will immediately issue a **free priority reprint** or a full refund to your original payment method.

## 14-Day Change of Mind Returns
Under UK statutory law, you have 14 calendar days from receipt of goods to request a return.
- Items must be unused, unmounted, and in their original packaging.
- The buyer is responsible for return postage via a tracked postal service.
- Once inspected, your refund will be processed within 3-5 business days.
