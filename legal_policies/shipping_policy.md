# UK Shipping & Delivery Policy

Thank you for shopping with Heritage Fine Art UK. Here is everything you need to know about our dispatch and delivery procedures.

## Production & Dispatch Time
All of our fine art prints are made to order at our specialist UK print studio using 12-colour giclée printing on 200gsm archival matte paper.
- **Production Time:** 24 - 48 hours (excluding weekends and UK bank holidays).
- **Dispatch Location:** Hampshire / Surrey, United Kingdom.

## UK Domestic Delivery Times & Rates
| Delivery Method | Courier Service | Delivery Window | Rate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard UK Tracked** | Royal Mail 48 Tracked | 2 - 3 Business Days | **FREE** (Orders over £30) / £2.95 |
| **Express UK Delivery** | Royal Mail 24 / DPD Tracked | 1 - 2 Business Days | £4.95 |

## Protective Packaging
All prints (A3, A2, A1) are rolled in acid-free glassine tissue paper and placed inside heavy-gauge, rigid cardboard postal tubes with sealed plastic caps to protect against creasing, moisture, and courier handling. A4 prints are shipped flat in rigid reinforced mailers.

## Tracking Your Parcel
Once your order is handed over to Royal Mail, you will receive an automatic email and SMS containing your Royal Mail tracking code and live tracking link.
