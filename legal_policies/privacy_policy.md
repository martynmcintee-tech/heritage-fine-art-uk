# Privacy Notice (UK GDPR Compliant)

This Privacy Notice outlines how Heritage Fine Art UK collects, uses, and protects personal data in compliance with the UK Data Protection Act 2018 and UK GDPR.

## Information We Collect
- **Contact Details:** Name, billing address, shipping address, email address, phone number.
- **Transaction Details:** Order history, items purchased, payment status (card details are processed via PCI-DSS compliant Shopify Payments/Stripe; we never store your full card number).
- **Technical Data:** IP address, browser type, cookies for checkout functionality.

## How We Use Your Data
- To fulfill and deliver orders via our UK print partners (Prodigi Group Ltd) and Royal Mail.
- To send transactional notifications (order confirmation, tracking updates).
- To detect and prevent fraudulent transactions.

## Third-Party Data Processors
We share minimal necessary data with:
1. **Prodigi Group Ltd (UK):** Name, shipping address, and order SKU for printing and packing.
2. **Royal Mail / DPD:** Shipping address and tracking information for delivery.
3. **Shopify Inc:** E-commerce platform and secure checkout processing.

You have the right to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of your personal data at any time by contacting our Data Privacy Officer.
